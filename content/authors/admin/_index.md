Hello there! My name is **Álvaro Cabrera**, a **PhD candidate in Sociology** at the **University of Michigan, Ann Arbor**. I hold a Bachelor's degree in History and a Master's in Social Science from Universidad de Chile, as well as a Master's in Latin American Studies from the University of Wisconsin-Madison. I am from Santiago, Chile.
{style="text-align: justify;"}

My **[research interests](/research/)** are broadly aligned with economic sociology, political sociology, political economy, and labor. Substantively, I am interested in social classes, capitalism as an economic system, production and competition under capitalist relations of production, the history of capitalism, and more.
{style="text-align: justify;"}
